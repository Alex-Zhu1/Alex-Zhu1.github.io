Biography

Hi, I’m Haiming Zhu. My research interests lie in Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and Generative Models. Previously, I worked on video, image, and 3D scene editing by leveraging diffusion model priors with Prof. He and Dr. Xu. More recently, I have become interested in Embodied AI, where I am exploring research on hand–object interaction (HOI) with Prof. Zhao.
